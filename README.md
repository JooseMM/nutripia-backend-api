### 1. Basic Measures
These are used for general description and the calculation of mass and proportionality indices.
* **Body mass:** Total weight of the individual in kilograms (**kg**).
* **Stature:** Maximum height from the floor to the vertex of the head.
* **Sitting height:** Height from the seat to the vertex.
* **Arm span:** Maximum distance between the middle fingers with arms extended.

---

### 2. Skinfolds
Used to estimate subcutaneous adiposity (body fat). Measured in millimeters (**mm**).
* **Triceps:** On the posterior aspect of the arm.
* **Subscapular:** Below the inferior angle of the scapula.
* **Biceps:** On the anterior aspect of the arm.
* **Iliac Crest:** Immediately superior to the iliac crest.
* **Supraspinale:** At the intersection of the iliac crest mark and the axillary fold line.
* **Abdominal:** 5 cm to the right of the ombilicus.
* **Front thigh:** At the midpoint of the anterior aspect of the thigh.
* **Medial calf:** On the inner aspect of the leg, at the level of maximum girth.

---

### 3. Girths (Circumferences)
Measures the circumference of body segments to assess muscle mass and tissue distribution.
* **Head:** Maximum girth above the eyebrows.
* **Neck:** Just above the Adam's apple (laryngeal prominence).
* **Arm (relaxed):** Midpoint between the acromion and the radius.
* **Arm (flexed and tensed):** Maximum circumference with the biceps flexed.
* **Forearm:** Maximum girth distal to the elbow.
* **Wrist:** Minimum girth distal to the styloid processes.
* **Chest:** At the level of the fourth chondrosternal joint (mesosternale).
* **Waist:** At the narrowest point between the last rib and the iliac crest.
* **Hip:** At the maximum protrusion of the buttocks.
* **Thigh (1 cm):** 1 cm below the gluteal fold.
* **Thigh (mid):** At the midpoint between the greater trochanter and the superior border of the patella.
* **Calf:** Maximum girth of the leg.
* **Ankle:** Minimum girth above the malleolus.
